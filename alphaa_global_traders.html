HOW EXPORTING WORKS

01

ENQUIRY & SPEC

Submit product specifications, destination port, incoterms (FOB/CIF), and requested order volume.

02

LAB TESTING & LOT

Pre-shipment batch inspection, fumigation certification, SGS / Spice Board testing, and barcode packaging.

03

PORT CUSTOMS

Port gate-in, shipping bill lodgement, EDI clearance, and container stuffing at bonded CFS yards.

04

BILL OF LADING

Vessel departure, original negotiable BL issued, complete bank documentation dispatched via DHL express.
